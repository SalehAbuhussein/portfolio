## Direction contract
THESIS: The career set as one strict Swiss typographic poster; refuses the photo-beside-headline hero over a row of cards.
OWN-WORLD: Uncoated off-white stock (#f2f1ec), black ink, one red (#e3262b) for rules, the contact field and focus. Archivo grotesque, flush left, ragged right, 12-column grid, 1px black rules, no cards, no shadows, no rounded corners.
STORY: In seconds the visitor sees name, "Angular frontend developer at Motory", and that he also ships ASP.NET Core + SQL Server; scans numbered experience and projects; emails him.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Name at max display size across the grid, thick red rule under it, then three columns: current role, the .NET claim, contact with email as the primary action; grayscale avatar in a single grid cell.
FORM: Swiss grid poster, candidate 5 of 7, seed key 7ea30243. Raises: numbered margin column (orizuru), identical skill cells as ruler (VU bridge), every section and project anchor-linkable (HyperCard).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
